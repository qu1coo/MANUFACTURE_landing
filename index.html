Frequently Asked Questions

We respond to what our clients are usually interested in

What is the minimum order quantity (print run)?

We accept orders for wholesale tailoring from 50 units per model in one color. This is the optimal volume, allowing You to keep the cost of production favorable for You.

Can I order a test sample before launching the batch?

Yes, of course! We will definitely sew the signal sample(s) so that you can evaluate the quality of the cut, fabric and print. Only after your approval will we launch the entire edition.

Do you work only with your own fabric or can you bring your own?

We work according to both schemes. We have proven Turkish suppliers of high-quality canvas (penye, compact penye), but we are also ready to sew products from Your raw materials.

Do you help with the development of patterns?

Yes, our in-house design technologist develops patterns of any complexity based on Your references, photographs or sketches. We can also modify Your ready-made patterns to meet the standards of our equipment.
